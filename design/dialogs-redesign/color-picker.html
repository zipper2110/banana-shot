Color picker. One dark dialog in place of the Swing JColorChooser. Three buttons open it: the player colors (Scoring settings), the accent color (Scoreboard style) and the comment color (Points). It keeps all parts of JColorChooser: the swatches, the recent colors, the HSV, HSL, RGB and CMYK values, the hex code, the preview of the current and the new color, and Reset. Click "Current" to use the current color again.
