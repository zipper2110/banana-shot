Scoring tab — redesign proposals

This folder has one main proposal and two alternatives for the Scoring tab. Each mockup is an HTML page with the app sidebar, a frame of the sample video with the scoreboard preview, and 128 points. The first 44 points have a score. The mockups work: use the keys Q, W, E, R, Shift+R, A, S, F, Space, the arrow keys, and click the buttons and the list. Use the switches at the top of each page to see manual scoring, the serve marks and the first-visit balloon.

✓ Cosmetic changes only. No feature is added or removed.

The final design uses option C as the base, with these changes from the review:

  • Playback bar: the current time goes away. The scrub times count from the point start ("0:00.0" to "0:07.6").

  • Manual scoring: sets and games have a "+" toggle button instead of the icon tags. Points also get a "+" button. It records the point for that player, the same as Q and E.
  • The score column caption "Pts" becomes "Points".
  • Points list: rows are 40 px high. The "Won by" column goes away; the color strip shows the winner. A "Go to point" button (icon only) shows on hover, to the left of the star. It opens the Points tab and selects that point there. In the mockup, it opens the Points final mockup at that point.

All three options put steps 2 and 3 next to each other. The next step has the lime outline, as in the Points final design.

Problems in the current tab

  1. The flow is split over the screen. "Point for Player 1" is at the bottom left, "No point" is in the center, "Point for Player 2" is at the bottom right. "Next Point" is at the bottom of the list, in the other corner of the window.
  2. The score is split. The POINTS values are in the header rows. GAMES and SETS are two cards for each player, at the left and at the right edge. To read "5–1, 15–0" you must look at four places.
  3. Two scores without captions. The scoreboard on the video shows the score before the point. The bottom panel shows the score after the point. Nothing tells the user about this difference.
  4. Markers look like buttons. In automatic scoring, "Game Won" and "Set Won" look like buttons, but a click does nothing. Only the cursor and the tooltip show the difference from manual scoring.
  5. The serve button is hard to find. It is a small racket icon without text next to the point button. Its three states differ only by the color and a lime border.
  6. The list rows are busy. A row has the outcome strip, a check or an empty ring, a serve racket, a GAME or SET badge and a star. The check repeats the strip. The row does not say who won.
  7. The footer mixes two kinds of actions. Next Point, Previous Point, Scoring Settings and Scoreboard Style are four full-width buttons with the same style.
  8. Long key text. "Point for Player 1 [Q]", "Previous Point [Shift+R]", "-5s [shift+←]", "Seek frame-by-frame [F]" and the "Up / Down speed" label use text for the keys.
  9. Small captions. "Point segment" is 9 px. "0 Total", "0 Scored" and "0 Fav" are small pills with abbreviations.
  10. Different from the Points tab. The Points final design has the list on the right and the key chips on the buttons. The Scoring tab has the list on the left and no key chips.

Design principles

One score, one placeSets, games and points of both players are in one table, with a caption that says "after the point".
Decide, then go onWho won and Next point are next to each other. Unscored point: the outcome buttons have the lime outline. Scored point: Next point is lime.
States look like statesIn automatic scoring, "Game" and "Set" are tags that show only when this point won them. In manual scoring, they are dashed toggle buttons.
Serve in wordsThe serve button says "Serves" for the server. A pin shows a mark on this point.
Same parts as PointsKey chips, the playback bar with SPACE under the play button, the list header and the count line come from the Points final design.

Please confirm

These changes show more of the current data. They add no function, but you can remove them.
  • The point bar shows the current time and the position in the point ("0:04.2 of 0:07.3 in the point"). Now the scrub bar shows only the start and the end.
  • The list shows the winner name in a "Won by" column. Now only the color strip shows it.
  • The list shows the length with one decimal ("7.3 s"). Now it shows whole seconds ("7s").
  • A thin bar shows the scored points of all points. The app calculates it from the current counts.
  • The desk caption says "Score after this point". Option C also shows the start time and the length of the point in the panel header.
  • The leading player's points value is lime.

Text changes

  • "Played Points" becomes "Points". "0 Total", "0 Scored", "0 Fav" become "44 of 128 scored" and "43 favorites".
  • "Point for Player 1 [Q]" becomes the player name with a key chip "Q" (A, C) or "Point for Alex" with a key chip (B). The tooltip "Q — Point for Alex" stays.
  • "No point [W]" becomes "No point" with a key chip "W".
  • "Next Point [R]" becomes "Next point" with a key chip "R". "Previous Point [Shift+R]" becomes "Previous" with the key chips "⇧" and "R".
  • "Game Won" and "Set Won" become "Game" and "Set" (A), "Game won" and "Set won" (B), or icons (C).
  • The serve button gets the text "Serves" for the server. The tooltips stay the same.
  • "Point segment" goes away. The scrub bar gets the captions "Point start" and "Point end".
  • "-5s [shift+←]" and the other seek labels become "−5s" with key chips. "Seek frame-by-frame [F]" becomes "Frame step" with a key chip. "Up / Down speed" becomes "Speed" with the key chips ↑ and ↓. The tooltips stay the same.
  • "Scoring Settings" and "Scoreboard Style" become "Scoring settings" and "Scoreboard style".

Notes

  • The colors come from UiStyles and PlayerControls: DARK_BG, CARD_BG, LIME, YELLOW, the default player colors #4DA3FF and #FF6B6B, and SERVE_BUTTON_COLOR.
  • The score comes from a port of ScoringEngine.timeline with the default rules: best of 3 sets, 6 games, tiebreak at 6–6 to 7 points, advantage. The serve logic and the manual marks follow the same rules.
  • The scoreboard on the video is a simple drawing of the Broadcast style. The mpv player paints the real scoreboard. It is the export preview, not a Swing control.
  • The Scoring settings and Scoreboard style buttons open a stub. The dialogs are not part of this proposal.
  • frame.jpg is the same frame as in design/points-redesign. The points are the same as in the Points mockups. The outcomes are generated.
  • The mockups use the Material Symbols font from Google Fonts for the icons. Without internet access, the icons show as words.
  • Add #scoring=manual&serve=marked&hint=on&point=31 to a page URL to open it in a given state. Add points=none to see the empty list.
