Transform tab — redesign proposals

This folder has one main proposal and two alternatives for the Transform tab. Each mockup is an HTML page with the app sidebar, the real texts and a frame of the reference video. The crop editor on the video works: drag the rectangle, the square handles and the round handle, or click the video and use the arrow keys. The sliders and the value fields work too. Use the switch at the top of each page to see adjusted values.

✓ Cosmetic changes only. No feature is added or removed.

Problems in the current tab

  1. Too much empty space. Each slider row is 92 px high, with a gap of 42 px under the slider. Five sliders use about 460 px of the panel, and the rest of the panel is empty.
  2. No groups. The five sliders are one list with the same weight. Zoom, Pan X and Pan Y set the crop rectangle. Rotation and Fine Rotation set the angle. The list does not show this.
  3. The default is not visible. Each slider fills from its left end. The default of Pan and Rotation is the center, and the default of Zoom (100%) is at 23% of the track. No slider has a mark at the default, so the user cannot see at one look which values are changed.
  4. The angle is in two parts. Rotation and Fine Rotation each show their own value. The angle that the video uses is the sum of the two values, but the tab does not show it.
  5. Units and directions are not clear. "deg" is a separate label after the field. Pan X and Pan Y have no unit and no direction: the user cannot know that a positive Pan Y moves the crop up. A changed value and a default value have the same color.
  6. The editor on the video is hidden. Move, resize, rotate, the Shift snap and the arrow keys are only in the Help. The arrow keys work only after a click on the video, and the tab does not show when the video has the keyboard focus.
  7. The playback controls use two rows. The play button is alone on a centered row above the seek bar. The Colors tab now has a one-row playback bar, so the two Video tabs are different.
  8. Reset does not tell its scope. "Reset" is a default button. Its text does not tell that it resets all transform values.

Design principles

The default is the referenceEach slider fills from its default: 100% for Zoom, 0 for the others. A tick marks the default. A changed slider has a lime thumb and a lime value.
Groups by purposeCrop: Zoom, Pan X, Pan Y. Rotation: Rotation, Fine rotation, and the total angle.
One angleThe Rotation group shows the angle that the video uses: Rotation + Fine rotation.
The editor tells its rulesThe mouse and key hints from the Help are visible next to the video. A chip shows when the arrow keys move the crop.
Same as ColorsThe header, the Reset all button, the group cards and the playback bar are the same as in the Colors tab.

Options

A · Grouped panel

Recommended

The layout stays the same: the video on the left and a 400 px panel on the right. The panel has a header with the changed count and "Reset all", two group cards, and a card with the mouse and key hints. It is the same structure as the new Colors tab.

  • The rows are 80 px high, not 92 px, and have no empty gap. All five sliders and the hints fit without a scroll bar.
  • Each row shows the label, a value field with the unit inside, the slider with a default tick, and the end labels (Left / Center / Right, Down / Center / Up).
  • The Rotation caption shows the total angle, for example "Angle +2.3°". The Rotation slider has small ticks at −90° and +90°.
  • A lime bar at the left edge marks each changed row.
  • A chip on the video tells the user to click the video for the arrow keys. After the click, the chip shows the keys, and the video gets a lime frame.
  • The playback bar is the same as in the Colors tab: the play button with SPACE, the current time, the seek bar and the video length.

B · Narrow panel with video labels

Alternative

The panel is 320 px wide, not 400 px, so the video gets more width. The rows are compact, and the groups are plain captions, not cards. The video shows more information: a value label under the crop rectangle and a hint strip at the bottom.

  • The video is about 7% wider than in option A (1200 px, not 1120 px, in a 1600 px window). A wider video makes the crop more precise.
  • The label under the crop rectangle shows the zoom and the angle, for example "140% · +2.3°". Its border turns lime while the user drags.
  • The Rotation group starts with the total angle in large text.
  • Each row has an icon. The icon is lime when the value is changed.
Open option B →
Best for
Smaller screens, and users who work mostly on the video with the mouse.
Swing effort
Low to medium. The label and the hint strip are drawn on the video, so OverlayShape needs text and rounded rectangles.
Risk
Low to medium. The hint strip covers a part of the video. The panel width is different from the Colors tab (400 px).

C · Dial and ruler

Alternative

The layout stays the same. The crop sliders are one-line rows. Rotation is a dial, and Fine rotation is a ruler that moves under a center mark, as in the straighten tool of photo editors.

  • The dial shows the angle as an angle: the knob points in the direction of the rotation. Marks show 0°, ±90° and 180°, the snap angles of the round handle.
  • The ruler shows ±5° with a tick for each 0.1°. The user drags the ruler to the left for larger values.
  • Both controls keep the value field, the range, the steps (0.5° and 0.1°) and the slider keys: the arrow keys, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End.
  • The total angle is next to the dial.

Text changes

  • "Fine Rotation" becomes "Fine rotation" (sentence case, as the other labels). The tooltip "Fine Rotation" can stay.
  • "deg" becomes "°". The units "%" and "°" move into the value field.
  • "Reset" becomes "Reset all". The tooltip "Reset Transform" stays. The Help text "Use Reset to restore the transform controls." must use the new name.
  • New status text in the header: "All at default" or "2 of 4 changed". The four values are zoom, pan X, pan Y and the angle.
  • New group captions: "Crop" and "Rotation". New angle text: "Angle +2.3°". B and C add "Rotation + Fine rotation".
  • New end labels: 10% / 400%, Left / Center / Right, Down / Center / Up, −180° / 0° / +180°, −5° / 0° / +5°.
  • New hints: "Drag inside the rectangle to move it.", "Drag a square handle to resize. The aspect ratio and the center stay.", "Drag the round handle to rotate. Hold Shift to snap to 90°.", "Click the video, then ← ↑ → ↓ move 1 px. Shift moves 10 px." It is the same information as the Transform help.
  • New chip on the video (A and C): "Click the video to move the crop with the arrow keys". After the click: "← ↑ → ↓ move 1 px · Shift 10 px".

Notes

  • The colors come from UiStyles: DARK_BG, CARD_BG, LIME, GREEN and ACCENT_TEXT.
  • The crop editor in the mockups is a port of CropEditorOverlay and CropGeometryMath. The frame turns with CSS. It is not the mpv preview.
  • The mockups use the Material Symbols font from Google Fonts for the icons. Without internet access, the icons show as words.
  • Add #preset=adjusted to a page URL to open it with adjusted values.
