Option C · Dial and ruler. The crop sliders are one-line rows. Drag the dial to set Rotation (steps of 0.5°). Drag the ruler to set Fine rotation (steps of 0.1°). The dial has marks at 0°, ±90° and 180°, the snap angles of the round handle. Arrow keys, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End work as on a slider.
