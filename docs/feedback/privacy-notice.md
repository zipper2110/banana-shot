# Privacy notice: feedback reports (maintainer notes)

The full text of the notice is on the landing site (B-10):
`site/public/privacy/index.html`, section "Feedback reports" (`#feedback`).
The URL of the page is `https://banana-shot-editor.app/privacy/`. Since
2026-10-03 (B-10 decision 4), the site page is the only full text. Change the
text there, not in this file.

The Privacy page in More of the app has a short version of this text
(`PrivacyPage.kt`). Keep the two texts in agreement.

When the report contract (`feedback-contract`) gets a new field, or the
retention of the Worker changes, update the "Feedback reports" section of the
site page.

- Since B-11 E2 (2026-10-19), a report also has the app language and the
  display language of the operating system (`app_language`, `os_language`).
  The site page and the Privacy page in the app name them.
- Since B-13 (2026-11-03), the site page and the texts of the app say "the
  operating system" or "the system" in place of "Windows", because the app
  also runs on macOS. The data did not change, so the notice version of the
  usage statistics stays 3.
