## Short description

Free Windows app that turns a full tennis or padel match recording into a
video with only the points and a scoreboard.

## Description

BananaShot is a free tennis and padel match video editor for Windows 10 and
11.

Open a recording from any phone or camera, mark where each point starts,
and click the player who won it. The app cuts the dead time between the
points, keeps score by the tennis or padel rules, and adds a scoreboard to
the video. A two-hour match often becomes a 30-minute video.

Features:

- Export the full match, only the points, or only your favorite points
  (highlights).
- 19 scoreboard styles. You can change the position, size, colors, and
  title.
- Match statistics card, set summaries, and a momentum chart.
- Comments on the video.
- Color correction, rotation, and crop, with change points when the light
  changes.
- YouTube thumbnail maker.
- Opens MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, M4V, and WMV, including 4K 60fps. A 50 GB match
  opens in about a second.
- Padel scoring with the golden point, the star point, and Americano.
- Works offline. No account, no upload, no watermark, and no ads.

You mark the points yourself, so any camera angle, light, or zoom works,
and the score is always correct.
