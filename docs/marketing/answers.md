# Ready answers to frequent questions

Phase 0 of `strategy.md`, the "prepare a short answer" item. Written on
2026-10-14.

Use these answers in chats, in Reddit and forum threads, and in YouTube
comments. The answers are plain spoken English, not Simplified Technical
English. Change them to your own words. Do not paste the same text in many
places.

Each answer agrees with the site (FAQ and pricing page). If the site
changes, update this file.

Replace each `[X]` with a real number from your own match.

## "Is there a Mac version?"

> Not yet, it's Windows only for now. A Mac version is planned. Want me to
> tell you when it's ready?

- Write each Mac request in the contact sheet. The count decides when to
  start B-13.

## "Why does each version stop after 6 months? What's the catch?"

> Each version works for 6 months, then you update for free with one click.
> The app warns you 30 days before, and your projects stay. It means
> everyone has the latest fixes, and I don't have to support old versions.
> Right now everything is free, no account, no ads, no watermark.

- Do not promise that the app stays free forever. The pricing page says
  only "as free as possible".

> I tried the AI apps, and I spent more time fixing wrong cuts and wrong
> scores than marking the points myself. Here you press one key when the
> point starts and one when it ends, so every cut and every score is right.
> My [X1] match took me about [X3] to mark.

- Lead with the result, not with "No AI".

## "Windows says 'Windows protected your PC'. Is it a virus?"

> No, it's just new. Windows trusts a new app only after many people
> download it. The installer is signed, so you can see my name as the
> publisher. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". The source code is
> public on GitHub too.

## "Does it work for padel / pickleball / other sports?"

> Padel, yes, since version 1.1. It knows golden point, star point, and
> Americano. Pickleball and other sports work with plain points (no real
> pickleball score yet). What do you play?

- Write each pickleball request in the contact sheet.

## "Does it upload my videos?"

> No. Everything happens on your PC, and it works offline. A 4K 60fps
> recording of a full match opens in about a second, because nothing is
> uploaded.
