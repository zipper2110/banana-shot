# Tutorial video: the recording kit

Phase 0 of `strategy.md`, the tutorial video item. Written on 2026-10-08.

The tutorial is the main asset for Phase 1. Each message to a friend, a
YouTuber, a coach, or a forum links to it. It answers "how much work is it?"
before people ask.

This file has:

1. The goal and the rules of the video.
2. The preparation.
3. The shot list with the spoken text.
4. The YouTube title, description, and thumbnail.
5. New description lines for your two match videos.

The spoken text is plain spoken English, not Simplified Technical English.
Change it to your own words. Read it aloud once before you record.

Replace each `[X]` with a real number from your own match. Do not use a
number that you did not measure.

## 1. Goal and rules

- Length: 2.5 to 3.5 minutes. People leave long tutorials.
- One message: "From a 2-hour recording to a match video with a scoreboard,
  in four steps."
- Show the result first (first 10 seconds). Then show how.
- Show the keys on the screen. Fast keys are the main reason that manual
  marking is fast. A key overlay proves it.
- Use one real match. Do not show a fake or perfect case.
- Talk to one person, as to a tennis friend.

## 2. Preparation (about 30 minutes)

### Software

- Screen recorder: OBS Studio (free). Record at 1920 × 1080, 30 or 60 fps.
- Key overlay: a free keystroke display, for example Carnac or KeyCastOW.
  Put it in a bottom corner, where it does not cover the app.
- Microphone: any headset is better than the laptop microphone. Record in a
  quiet room.
- Windows: turn on "Do not disturb", so that no notification shows.

### Projects

Prepare three things before you record. Then the video does not wait for
slow steps.

1. A new match video file that you can import (the full recording).
2. A project of the same match with all points marked and scored. Use it to
   jump ahead after you show the first points.
3. A finished export of this match with the scoreboard. Use it for the
   result at the start and at the end.

### Measure your real numbers

Write them down. You need them for the spoken text and the description.

- `[X1]` The length of the full recording, for example "1 hour 52 minutes".
- `[X2]` The length of the export with only the points.
- `[X3]` Your time to mark and score the whole match.
- `[X4]` The file size and format of the recording, for example "4K 60fps,
  48 GB". Record the tutorial with your biggest real file.

### App settings

- Close other projects. Use a clean projects list, or rename old projects.
- Use the scoreboard style that looks best on your video.
- Use player names that you can show. Ask your partners, or use their first
  names only with their permission.

### Recording tips

- Record each shot as a separate clip. Then a mistake costs only one shot.
- Move the mouse slowly. Stop the mouse while you talk.
- Use the "Only points" export of the same match for shots 1 and 9.
- If you speak with an accent, that is fine. A real player is more
  convincing than a perfect voice. Add English subtitles (YouTube can make
  them; check them before you publish).

### Editing

- Cut the pauses. Keep the pace fast.
- Do not add music under the voice, or keep it very quiet.
- Add the site address as text in shot 9:
  `banana-shot-editor.app`.
