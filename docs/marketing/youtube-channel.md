## Description

YouTube Studio > Customization > Basic info > Description. The limit is
1000 characters. The channel page shows only the first line before "more",
so the first line tells what the app does.

```
BananaShot is a free Windows app that turns a tennis recording into a video that's easy to watch and share. You can mark the points, keep score, and export the match with a scoreboard. The time between the points is gone.

On this channel, you can find:
- Tutorials for the app
- Full matches and highlights made with BananaShot
- New features when they come out

About the app:
- Free, with no account, no ads, and no watermark
- Works offline, so your video stays on your PC
- No AI: you decide each cut, and the score is always right
- Windows 10 and 11 (a Mac version is planned)

I made BananaShot for my own matches. If you have an idea or a question, write a comment. I read every one.

Download: https://banana-shot-editor.app/
```
