# 1.4.0

The items of `backlog.md` that are done in this release.

### B-59 Overlays on videos with non-square pixels

- Found on 2026-10-30 in the 1.3.0 smoke test (step 6). The fixture
  `ui-smoke.mp4` is 720x486 with a sample aspect ratio of 40:33. A player
  shows it at about 873x486. The scoreboard is 21% too wide in the export
  and in the Points preview.
- Cause: `AssOverlayWriter.kt` sets `PlayResX` to the stored width. It does
  not use the sample aspect ratio. Thus, the player stretches the overlay
  with the video.
- Users with DV, HDV (1440x1080), or broadcast videos get the problem.
  Phone and action camera videos have square pixels and are correct.
- Probably also in 1.2.0 (not checked). It does not block 1.3.0.
- The fix uses the display size of the video for the overlay. The video
  keeps its sample aspect ratio.
- Done when: on a video with non-square pixels, the scoreboard, the
  comments, the replay badge, and the statistics card have the same shape
  as on a video with square pixels, in the export and in the preview.
- Fixed on 2026-10-30:
  - ffprobe reads `sample_aspect_ratio` of the source. The export
    resolution and the render job keep it (`SampleAspect`). A job from an
    older build has square pixels.
  - The ASS files of the scoreboard, the comments, the replay badge, and
    the statistics card use the display width as `PlayResX` (873 for the
    fixture). The statistics card pages have the display aspect ratio.
  - The `subtitles` filter gets `original_size=<display width>x<height>`.
    Without it, libass makes the drawings narrower, but not the text.
  - A video with change points (B-47) keeps the sample aspect ratio of the
    source (`setsar=40/33`). Before, `setsar=1` squeezed the video.
  - `NonSquarePixelExportTest` exports `ui-smoke.mp4` with the real ffmpeg.
    It measures a square and a text in the stored frame.
- The Points preview needs no change in the code. mpv draws the overlay in
  window pixels, and the video area has the display aspect ratio. Check
  the preview again in the next smoke test (step 6).
- Not changed: a rotation on the Transform tab turns the stored pixels. On
  a video with non-square pixels, a large angle can change the shape of
  the video a little.
