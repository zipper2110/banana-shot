Changelog

The changes in each version. The newest version is at the top. The files of each version are on the GitHub releases page.

1.4.0

Release date: 2026-11-03

BananaShot is now also on Mac

BananaShot now works on Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. It is the same app, with all features of the Windows version. It is free on Mac too. Download for Mac

  • The export uses the video encoder of the Mac (VideoToolbox).
  • Press Cmd+? to open Help. The Help menu of the menu bar has the same item. F1 also works.
  • Cmd+Q saves the open project, as the close button does. About BananaShot in the app menu opens More → About.
  • Your projects and settings are in the folder ~/Library/Application Support/BananaShot.
  • Update and restart opens the installer of the new version. If you moved the app out of the Applications folder, the update installs the new version in the Applications folder, and the app offers to move the old copy to the Trash.

Select when the scoreboard shows

In Scoreboard settings → When to show, you can select when the scoreboard starts and ends in the "Full video" export:

  • Start: First point (the default), Video start, or Fixed time.
  • End: Right after the last point, 5, 10, or 20 s after the last point (the default), Until the video ends, or Fixed time.
  • Fixed time: type the time (hh:mm:ss), or click Use the current position. A fixed time belongs to the video of the project, so a new project does not get it.
  • When the start or the end hides the scoreboard on some points, a short hint tells which setting does it.
  • With a trim, "Video start" and "Until the video ends" mean the start and the end of the trim.
  • The "Only points" and "Only favorites" exports do not use these settings.

While you mark the points of a match that has not ended, the preview now keeps the scoreboard. A change of the settings shows in the preview at once.

Select the font of the scoreboard

In Scoreboard settings → Font, you can select the font of the scoreboard: Inter, Liberation Sans, Open Sans, Fira Sans, Gelasio, Cascadia Mono, or Caveat. Font of the style keeps the fonts of the scoreboard style.

The scoreboards, the statistics card, and the comments now use fonts that come with the app, so an export looks the same on each computer. A new export of a project from an earlier version can look a little different.

Statistics

  • Games won, Sets won, and Most games in a row come from the score. A game that you type with Override score now counts, also when no marked point wins it.
  • When the score has overrides, the Stats tab shows a note, because some statistics can then be wrong.
  • The statistics card of the video shows the share of the points and the games that each player won, as the Stats tab does.

Trim

  • A strip at the top of Colors & Transform has the sub-tabs Trim, Colors, and Transform. The tab opens on Trim.
  • Under the video, a timeline shows the whole video. Drag the two handles to set the start and the end. The parts outside the trim are hatched, and the points outside the trim are red.
  • A card for each end shows its time, with Start at the current position or End at the current position, and Go.

Other changes

  • The app asks before it deletes a point: with the Delete key, with the Delete button of a row, and in the Edit dialog.
  • Esc cancels the pending point. A change of the project also cancels it.
  • Each point ends with its winner, so the "not scored" state and the V key are gone. The Projects tab shows a "Points" column.
  • An export cannot replace the source video. The Export tab then asks for another file.
  • A "Full video" export with a statistics card now also works when the audio of the video file is longer than the video. Many phones and cameras make such files.
  • When the saved place of the window is not on a connected screen, the window opens in the middle of the main screen.
  • The texts say "computer" in place of "PC".

1.3.1

Release date: 2026-10-31

  • Trim: a new Trim sub-tab on Colors & Transform sets the start and the end of the video. The "Full video" export uses only this part. The app keeps the points outside the trim and shows them in red.
  • Full-video export: the scoreboard starts at the first point and shows the final score for 20 seconds after the last point, as in the preview.
  • Videos with non-square pixels (for example DV or HDV): the scoreboard, the comments, the replay badge, and the statistics card keep their shape.

1.3.0

Release date: 2026-10-30

  • Languages: the app is now also in Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Japanese, and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). It uses the language of Windows, or you can select one in More → Settings. The texts in the video can use a different language: select it in Export → Video language.
  • Instant replay: you can show a part of a point again right after the point, with a zoom and slow motion. A point can have more than one replay.
  • Comments: you can drag a comment on the video, and change its size, opacity, and rotation.
  • Thumbnail: 25 new styles in 5 groups. You can add your logo, the club name, the round, and the score.
  • Beach tennis: a new sport with the ITF rules, a match tiebreak in place of the third set, and no-ad.
  • Match end: after the match point, the scoreboard shows the final score of all sets, and the Points tab shows the match end. A new format "Sets, no match end" has no last set.
  • "What's new": from now on, the app shows the main changes when a new version is out.
  • Usage statistics also send the language of the app and of Windows. See the privacy notice.

1.2.0

Release date: 2026-10-17

  • Points: you can mark the points and score them on one tab. The Scoring tab is gone. With "Override score", you can type the score after a point. "Frame step" moves the video one frame at a time.
  • Colors & Transform: one tab for the colors and for the crop and rotation. If the light changes or the phone moves during the match, you can add a change point. The video blends to the new values in 1 second.
  • Thumbnail: a new tab makes a YouTube thumbnail from a frame of a point, with large texts and the match data. You can select a style, change the colors, and zoom into the frame.
  • Scoreboards: two new styles, Stadium and Stadium Compact.

1.1.0

Release date: 2026-10-11

  • Padel: score padel matches with two teams. The deuce can be advantage, golden point, or star point. Americano matches count a fixed total of points. The scoreboard shows the teams, and the statistics use a padel court.
  • Themes: you can select a dark, mid, or light theme and your own accent color in More → Settings.
  • Projects: when the match video is moved or renamed, you can find it again with "Locate video..." and continue the project.
  • Extended statistics (only if you turn them on) also send the theme, the language, and the sports of your projects. See the privacy notice.

1.0.0

Release date: 2026-10-06

The first public version.

  • Projects: import a match video and continue later from the list of recent projects.
  • Points: mark the start and the end of each point, mark favorites, and add comments to the video.
  • Scoring: give each point to a player. The app counts the score for many match formats and shows the server.
  • Statistics: serve, break points, point length, and a momentum chart. A statistics card and set summaries in the video.
  • Colors and Transform: light and color controls, zoom, move, and rotate.
  • Export: the full match, only the points, or only the favorites, with the scoreboard and the comments. Hardware encoders from NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel. An export queue.
  • Feedback form in the app. Anonymous usage statistics: essential statistics on by default (you can turn them off), and extended statistics only if you turn them on.
  • Update check and "Update and restart".
