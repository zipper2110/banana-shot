Contact

Send a problem, an idea, or a question. Each message goes to the author of BananaShot. Many answers are already in the FAQ.

Send a message

Topic

The author can reply only if you give an address. The reply comes from contact [at] banana-shot-editor.app.

The form sends only the topic, your message, and your email address. See the privacy notice.

Message sent

From the app

Click the Feedback button in the sidebar of the app. Select a topic, write your message, and click Send. Error messages also have a Report this problem button.

The form in the app can attach the log files. For a problem, this is the best way. Give your email address in the form if you want a reply.

Email

Write to contact [at] banana-shot-editor.app. The author replies from this address.

Report a problem by email

  • Tell what you did, what happened, and what you expected.
  • Add the app version and the version of Windows. The app shows its version in More → Contact.
  • Attach the log files. They are in the folder %APPDATA%\BananaShot\logs. More → Contact also has an Open log folder button.

The log files contain the names and folders of your videos and projects. They do not contain your videos. Open the files to see all the data before you send them.

Your data

The privacy notice tells what a feedback report contains and how long the data stays. To ask for a copy or a deletion of your data, write to the address above.
