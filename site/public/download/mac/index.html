Install

  1. Download BananaShot-osx-Setup.pkg and open it. The installer and the app are signed and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens them with no warning.
  2. Follow the steps of the installer. When it asks where to install, select Install for me only. Then you do not need an administrator password.
  3. The installer installs the app and starts it.

Where is the app? With "Install for me only", the app is in the Applications folder of your home folder (~/Applications), not in the Applications folder of the Finder sidebar. Spotlight and Launchpad find it. To keep it in the Dock, right-click its Dock icon, then select Options → Keep in Dock.

Updates

The app checks for a new version when it starts. When a new version is available, the app shows a notice. Click Update and restart. The app downloads the new version, installs it, and starts again. Your projects and settings stay.

The app opens the installer of the new version. Follow its steps as at the first install. Then the new version starts.

Uninstall

Quit BananaShot. Drag BananaShot from the Applications folder of your home folder (~/Applications) to the Trash. If you installed it for all users, it is in the Applications folder of the Finder sidebar.

Your projects and settings stay. They are in the folder ~/Library/Application Support/BananaShot. To remove them too, delete this folder. Your videos and exported videos stay where you saved them.
