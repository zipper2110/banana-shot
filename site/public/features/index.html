Mark and score

From a raw recording to a scored match

No dead time

Mark the points, and the export keeps only them. A two-hour match often becomes a 30-minute video.

  • One key starts a point, and one click ends it.
  • Mark the best points as favorites for a highlights video.
  • Frame step finds the exact moment.
  • Trim cuts off the warm-up and the time after the match.

Score with a click

Click the player who won each point. The app counts points, games, and sets by the rules of your sport.

  • Tennis, padel, and beach tennis, singles or doubles.
  • Many match formats: best of 3 or 5, a match tiebreak, short sets, and more. See the sports.
  • Mark who serves, and the scoreboard shows it.
  • Override score lets you type the score when you need to.

Match statistics

The app counts the statistics from your points: serve, break points, point length, runs of points, and a momentum chart.

  • See them for the match or for each set.
  • Pick the rows that go into the video.
In the video

More than the score

A scoreboard in the video

The score shows in the video the whole time. Pick one of 19 styles and make it yours.

  • Change the colors, the font, the title, and the position.
  • Show the player colors and the serve.
  • Select when the scoreboard shows in a full-video export.

Comments on the video

Leave a note on any moment, and it shows on the video.

  • Drag a comment to its place on the video.
  • Change its size, opacity, and rotation.
  • Pick a style: Plain, Shadow, Outline, Bold, Card, or Pill.

A statistics card

Add a statistics card at the end of the video, and a summary after each set. There are five card styles.

  • A compact card fits more rows on a page.
  • Group titles, and a light or a dark card.
  • The momentum chart shows how the match went.
Picture and thumbnail

A clear picture and a good first look

Colors and framing

Make a dark or flat video clear. Zoom, move, and rotate the image to frame the court.

  • Light and color controls, from brightness to temperature.
  • If the light changes or the camera moves during the match, add a change point. The video blends to the new values.

A thumbnail for YouTube

Make a thumbnail from a frame of your match, with large texts and the match data. There are more than 30 styles.

  • Add the players, the event, the round, and the score.
  • Add your logo and the club name.
  • Change the colors and zoom into the frame.
Export

See it first, then export

Export previewNew

Watch the result before the export starts, with the scoreboard, the comments, the replays, and the statistics cards.

  • Go to the next or the previous part with one key.
  • Click a part on the strip to go there.
  • Not happy? Change the settings and look again, with no long wait.

Fast and flexible export

Export the full match, only the points, or only your favorites. Keep the original quality or pick a smaller file.

  • Up to 4K at 60 fps.
  • The app uses the graphics card of your computer for a fast export.
  • More exports can wait in a queue.
  • The texts in the video can use a different language than the app.
And more

The small things that help

Instant replay

Show a part of a point again right after it, with a zoom and slow motion. A point can have more than one replay.

A match in several files

If your camera splits a match into several files, you can select them all when you make a project. They play as one video.

Your language

The app is in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Japanese, and Chinese.

Projects

Stop at any time and continue later from the list of projects. If you move the video, you can find it again.

Themes

Pick a dark, mid, or light theme and your own accent color.

Local and offline

The app edits on your computer and does not upload your videos. The original file stays as it is.

Get BananaShot

Free for Windows 10 and 11, and for Macs with Apple Silicon (macOS 15 or later). Opens MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, M4V, and WMV files.
