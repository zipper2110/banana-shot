How to make a padel match video with a scoreboard

By Dmitrii Litvin, the author of BananaShot. Updated on 2026-10-18.

A padel match video gets a scoreboard the same way as a tennis video: you mark each point and give it to the team that won it. The app must know the padel rules, like the golden point, the star point, or Americano. BananaShot can count all of them.

Padel rules in the app

  • Two teams. Type both players of a team in one name, for example "Lebrón / Galán".
  • The deuce. Advantage, golden point (the point at 40–40 wins the game), or star point (the first two deuces use advantage, and the point at the third deuce wins the game).
  • Match formats. Best of 3 sets, best of 3 with a super tiebreak, one set, a pro set to 8 or 9 games, short sets to 4 games, games only, or a single super tiebreak.
  • Americano. The points count 1, 2, 3 until both teams together played the total, for example 24 points. The team with more points wins.

Step by step with BananaShot

BananaShot is a free app for Windows 10 and 11. It edits on your PC, and your video stays there.

  1. Open your recording. The app opens MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, M4V, and WMV files, including 4K 60fps.
  2. Select padel. When you score the first point, the scoring settings open. Select the sport Padel, type the two teams, and select the match format and the deuce rule.
  3. Mark the points. At the start of a point, set the start with one key. At the end, click the team that won it.
  4. Choose the look. Click Scoreboard style. The Padel Glass style is made for padel, but you can use any of the 19 styles. During a golden point or a star point, the scoreboard can show a badge.
  5. Export the video. Select Scoreboard and export the full match, only the points, or the favorites. The statistics use a padel court.

Tips

  • A camera behind the back glass, high up, usually shows the whole court and all four players.
  • Mark the server of one point. The app then follows the serve and shows a ball next to the team that serves.
  • Read how to record a match with a phone. Most of it works for padel too.
