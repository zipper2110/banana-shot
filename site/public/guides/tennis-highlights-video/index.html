How to make a highlights video of a tennis match

By Dmitrii Litvin, the author of BananaShot. Updated on 2026-10-18.

A highlights video shows only the best points of a match. Go through the match once, mark the best points as favorites, and export only the favorites. With a scoreboard on the video, viewers can follow the match even when most points are cut.

What makes good highlights

  • Few points. Pick fewer points than you think. Long rallies, big serves on important points, and the last point of each set work well.
  • The real score. Viewers like to know what was at stake. Score every point of the match, not only the favorites. Then the scoreboard shows the real score at each highlight.
  • Some context. A short summary after each set and the match statistics at the end tell the story of the points that you cut.

Step by step with BananaShot

BananaShot is a free app for Windows 10 and 11. It edits on your PC, and your video stays there.

  1. Mark and score the points. Open your recording. Mark the start of each point, and click the player who won it at the end. See how to cut the dead time for the details.
  2. Mark the favorites. In the list of points, mark the best ones as favorites. Favorites show in yellow on the timeline.
  3. Export only the favorites. On the Export tab, select Only favorites. The card shows how long the video will be and how many points it has.
  4. Add the context. Select Scoreboard. You can also add Set summaries after each set and a Statistics card at the end of the video.
  5. Make a thumbnail. For YouTube, the Thumbnail tab can make a picture from a frame of a favorite point, with large words like "Best points" and the names of the players.

Tips

  • You can export the full match, only the points, and only the favorites from the same project. Make the highlights for your friends and the full video for your coach.
  • Mark the favorites the first time you go through the match. It's easier than to look for them again later.
  • If you post on YouTube, put the highlights and the full match in the same playlist.
