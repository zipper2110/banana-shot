Which sports can BananaShot score?

By Dmitrii Litvin, the author of BananaShot. Updated on 2026-10-26.

BananaShot can score tennis, padel, and beach tennis. You pick the sport when you create a project, and the app counts games, sets, and tiebreaks by the rules of that sport.

Tennis

Tennis is where BananaShot started. The deuce can be advantage or no-ad, where the point at 40–40 wins the game. You can pick best of 3 or 5 sets, best of 3 with a match tiebreak, one set, a pro set to 8 games, short sets to 4 games, games only, or a single tiebreak.

For doubles, you can type both players of a team in one name, for example "Dima / Yana".

Padel

A padel project has two teams. Besides advantage, the app knows the golden point and the star point, which the FIP rules use since 2026: the first two deuces use advantage, and the point at the third deuce wins the game. During a golden point or a star point, the scoreboard can show a badge.

The app can also count Americano matches. The points count 1, 2, 3 until both teams together played the total, for example 24 points. The Padel Glass scoreboard style is made for padel, but every sport can use it. The padel guide has the steps.

Beach tennis

Beach tennis is new in version 1.3.0. A beach tennis project has two teams, and it starts with the ITF rules: two sets with a tiebreak at 6–6, a 10-point match tiebreak in place of the third set, and no-ad at 40–40. For club games, you can switch to advantage or to another format, like one set or short sets.

Other sports

Would you like to see your sport in the app? You can tell us on the contact page.

Changing the sport

You pick the sport in the New project dialog, and you can change it later in the scoring settings on the Points tab. When the sport changes, the match format changes to the default of the new sport, and "Player 1" becomes "Team 1".

In every sport, you can also set your own rules under Custom scoring rules in the scoring settings: the number of sets, the games in a set, the tiebreaks, the deciding set, and the deuce.
